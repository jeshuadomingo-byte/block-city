# Block City

Block City is an original, mobile-first arcade game for players who want a
short open-world crime experience in a browser.

## First release

- A player can walk and enter a nearby vehicle.
- A player can complete a courier mission and collect a cash pickup.
- Police pursue the player after actions add heat.
- Touch controls work on a phone, and keyboard controls work on a desktop.
- The game saves the player's cash and completed-mission count.

## Acceptance checks

1. Start the game and display the city and HUD.
2. Move with arrow keys or touch controls.
3. Enter a marked vehicle and observe the speed change.
4. Visit the courier marker and complete the mission.
5. Collect cash and verify that the HUD balance increases.
6. Increase heat and verify that police vehicles begin pursuing.
7. Reload the page and verify saved cash and mission progress remain.
